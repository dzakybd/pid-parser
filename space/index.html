pid-parser

A P&ID image in, tables and a graph out. A vision-language model finds the symbols tile by tile; the connections are read from the pixels (no model); small scripts check the graph and export it for SQL or a graph database. No GPU, no training.

npx skills add dzakybd/pid-parser

Works in Claude Code, Codex and other skill-aware agents, or as a LangChain Deep Agent. Source: github.com/dzakybd/pid-parser. How it works, step by step: the write-up. The sheet is one very dense P&ID (190 symbols, 732 connected symbol pairs); the two tabs are two runs on it. These results were computed ahead of time; this page calls no model.
